# MTN Collections qualification and production blocker

SmartDuka has qualified the MTN MoMo Collections sandbox technical path: OAuth,
RequestToPay, provider status polling, event-ledger reconciliation, duplicate
protection, and customer payment-state reads.

This is not production marketplace approval. SmartDuka must not enable normal
customer MTN payments until MTN Uganda contractually and technically confirms a
model where each customer payment settles directly into the selected vendor's
merchant account, rather than SmartDuka's collection wallet. The direct-routing
environment gates remain false until that evidence exists.
